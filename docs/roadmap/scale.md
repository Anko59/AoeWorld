# Roadmap: performance and the scale ladder

Ambition (owner 10-26, restated 11-07, 11-13): 1000 players and 10M units on this one machine (12 cores, 14 GB),
minimum 1000p/1M, real time at every rung, no queue (units never silently wait), level of detail allowed,
sharding later and never required. Near target 11-08: about 2M units for the management hierarchy.
Rungs: 0 measurement, 1 100k units, 2 500k, 3 2M, 4 clients (see issue 748).

**Plan since 11-21: [#978](https://github.com/Anko59/AoeWorld/issues/978), scale roadmap v2** (stages R1 61-75k units,
R2 250k, R3 1M, R4 2M in real time on this machine). Its State comment holds the current step and the frozen
references; the rows below stay as the record of what #865 tried and are not worked any more.

**Route caps:** #1096 removed the world, player and slot budgets. #1122 reduced the cost of worker lookahead
and planner restarts. The remaining movement caps are tracked in [caps](caps.md); combat, economy, orders and
display caps have their own rows there.
