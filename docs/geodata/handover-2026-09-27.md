## Scope and contracts

The creator selects a WGS84 location, square physical extent, and compression
ratio, and prepares an AoE2-style circa-600 map. Local azimuthal-equidistant
projection handles map geometry; equal-area overlap handles historical
quantities. Geographic fidelity takes priority over competitive balance.

Tiles represent two game meters. Requests support 250 m through 10,000 km,
compression 1 through 10,000, and sparse virtual dimensions 64 through 262,144
tiles per side. Maximum dimensions establish a storage/sampling contract,
not finished RTS or dedicated-hardware performance. Economy, buildings,
combat, and naval units are outside this feature.

Keep modern observations, historical reconstruction, explicit corrections,
procedural resources/detail, and fallback data distinguishable. Modern water
is evidence, not proof of the same shoreline in 600 CE. HYDE's real inconsistent
cell at row 1670/column 1281 remains an explicit error, not silently valid zero.
Native art currently maps six terrain and four resource groups; snow/ice use
the documented dry-grass fallback and tree art is broadleaf.
